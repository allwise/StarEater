Star Eater - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

The short version: Star Eater does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

What the game keeps, and where

Your progress - stardust, skills, high scores and the initials you type in, saved seed keys and your settings - is saved on your device only. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

If Android's own backup is switched on for your phone, Android may include this game data in your device backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings; we never see it.

Purchases

The full game is unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment entirely: we never see your card details, name or address. The game only asks Google Play whether the unlock has been bought on your account, so it can turn the full game on (and back on after a reinstall). Google's handling of your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The game asks for one permission: vibration, for small taps of feedback during play. It can be switched off in the game's options. The game reads the motion sensor only if you turn on tilt steering, and only to steer; nothing from it is stored or sent anywhere.

Children

Star Eater does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that sends data off your device - for example online leaderboards - this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
