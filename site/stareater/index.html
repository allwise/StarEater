STAR EATER

Coming soon to Google Play

You are the o. You eat the *. The letters eat you.

Star Eater grew out of a tiny game that lived in a computer terminal: a little o chasing stars while the letters E and S chased the o. Walk off one edge of the screen and you came back on the other - and the letters never worked that out. That trick is still the heart of it. Everything else has grown up around it.

Every level is dealt by a slot machine: a way of moving plus up to four twists. Glide with momentum, ride a scroller, swing round a sun in orbit. Then add darkness, debris, a ticking clock, meteor showers, shy stars that run from you, a tail that grows with every bite, gravity wells that sling you across the screen - or edges that are suddenly solid for you and open for them.

What's inside

  • A 30-level campaign with three bosses you beat by out-eating them, and a skill tree that grows with every run.
  • Arcade Matrix and Arcade Flow - no skill tree, just pick-ups and three pockets of one-use tricks.
  • Endless - one level that never ends and never stops getting worse.
  • Seed keys - every run has a six-letter key. Share it, and a friend gets the very same run to beat.
  • A daily run - the same seed for everyone, every day.
  • Play your way - touch, tilt steering, or a keyboard; vibration and screen shake you can switch off.
